The Imperial March (Darth Vader's Theme)

"The Imperial March" (also titled "Darth Vader's Theme") is a musical theme present in the Star Wars franchise. It was composed by John Williams for the film Star Wars: Episode V – The Empire Strikes Back.

First premiered on April 29, 1980, three weeks before the opening of the film, it is one of the best-known symphonic movie themes in cinematic history and a classic example of a leitmotif—a recurring musical phrase associated with a specific character or idea.

Overview & Impact

The motif is heavily associated with Darth Vader and the Galactic Empire. It is primarily built around a martial brass melody set over a driving rhythm, evoking power, order, and looming conflict.
